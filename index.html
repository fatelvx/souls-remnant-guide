官方 wiki 提到的練功規則Leveling Rules From the Official Wiki

主動攻擊Aggro

怪會不會主動追你，取決於你的等級。等級夠高之後牠們就不會先動手，但碰到還是會受傷。

Whether monsters chase you depends on your level. Once you are high enough, they will not attack first, but you can still take damage if you run into them.

重生速度Respawn Speed

怪被清得越快，地圖的重生速度就會跟著變快，一個人練也一樣。

The faster you clear the monsters in a map, the faster the map respawns them, and this works the same when you level alone.

組隊經驗Party EXP

同一張地圖組隊會有額外的 Bonus EXP，經驗依照每個人的輸出分配。跟隊友的等級差超過 10 級，分到的經驗就會開始減少。

Grouping on the same map gives extra Bonus EXP, and EXP is shared according to each person's damage output. If a party member is more than 10 levels away from you, the EXP you receive starts to drop.

測試傷害Testing Your Damage

訓練木樁Target Dummy

集市中樞裡有訓練木樁，可以拿來測單體傷害。它有一億 HP、攻擊力是 0，打很久也不會倒，也不會反擊。滑鼠移到左下角的 DPS 計量上，會顯示峰值 DPS。換裝備或換 buff 之前先打一次記下數字，換完再打一次，就能看出差多少。

There is a Target Dummy in the Market Hub you can use to test single-target damage. It has 100 million HP and 0 attack, so it won't go down or hit back. Hover over the DPS meter in the bottom-left corner to see your peak DPS. Hit it once before you change gear or buffs and note the number, then again after, and you can see the difference.

位置：玩家實測。HP 與攻擊力：wiki。

Location: player-tested. HP and attack: wiki.

精華掉落表Essence Drop Table

每種精華都分成黯淡、一般、光輝三階。金框標的是那一階掉落率最高的怪。勾選「只看我打得到的怪」之後，就只會在你的等級加上可越級的範圍內比較，等級設定跟練功地圖分頁共用。

Each essence has three tiers: Dull, Normal, and Radiant. The monster with the highest drop rate for each tier is outlined in gold. When you tick "Only monsters I can fight," the list only compares monsters up to your level plus how many levels above you that you allow. The level setting is shared with the Leveling Maps tab.

鍊金轉換Alchemy Conversions

  • 3 個黯淡加 150 靈魂水晶，可以合成 1 個一般 鍊金 Lv30
  • 1 個一般加 100 靈魂水晶，可以拆成 2 個黯淡 鍊金 Lv5
  • You can combine 3 Dull essences with 150 Soul Crystals into 1 Normal essence Alchemy Lv30
  • You can split 1 Normal essence with 100 Soul Crystals into 2 Dull essences Alchemy Lv5

光輝精華目前只知道能從怪物身上掉。

Radiant essence is currently only known to drop from monsters.

用途Uses

讓裝備發光、多一個升級欄位的活力之魂（Energetic Soul），每種黯淡精華各要 40 個，需要鍊金 Lv15。

The Energetic Soul makes gear glow and adds one more upgrade slot. It takes 40 of each Dull essence and requires Alchemy Lv15.

副本寶箱Dungeon Chests

史萊姆花園 Lv10 和平原地城 Lv20 的銀寶箱、木寶箱，會開出各種黯淡精華。

Silver and wooden chests in Slime Garden Lv10 and Plains Dungeon Lv20 can drop various Dull essences.

    上架規則Listing Rules

    • 同時最多上架 10 件，可以在商城增加
    • 每件會放 7 天，沒賣掉會退回信箱
    • 只有直接購買，沒有競標
    • 買東西時背包滿了，會改寄到信箱
    • You can list up to 10 items at once, and you can increase this limit in the Cash Shop.
    • Each listing stays up for 7 days. If it doesn't sell, it returns to your mailbox.
    • Only direct purchases are available. There are no bids.
    • If the buyer's bag is full when a purchase is made, the item is sent to the buyer's mailbox instead.

    怎麼看行情Reading Market Prices

    • 價格紀錄：價格走勢圖，可以切換一天、一週、一個月或一年
    • 出售分頁：選一件物品，會顯示近 7 天的平均和最低成交價、目前在賣的數量，以及這週賣出了幾件
    • Home：熱門物品和最新上架
    • 搜尋：可以用價格、等級、屬性篩選，最多指定 4 個數值
    • Price History: A price chart, with a choice of one day, one week, one month, or one year.
    • Sell tab: Pick an item to see its average and lowest sale price over the past 7 days, how many are currently for sale, and how many sold this week.
    • Home: Popular items and the newest listings.
    • Search: You can filter by price, level, and attributes, and set up to 4 values.

    攤位（商人技能）Stall (Merchant Skill)

    • 從出生點牧羊人旁邊進入集市中樞入口，就可以擺攤
    • 攤位會展示你在拍賣行上架的東西，透過攤位賣出的手續費是 4%
    • 一次只能擺一個攤位，每次擺 72 小時
    • 攤位留言可以用 BBCode
    • Go from Spawn, next to the Shepherd, into the Market Hub Entrance to set up a stall.
    • The stall shows the items you have listed on the Auction House. Items sold through a stall have a 4% fee.
    • You can only have one stall at a time, and each one lasts 72 hours.
    • You can use BBCode in stall messages.

    元素效果Elements

    每把武器會隨機帶一種元素。標 * 的數值是以元素效果強度 100% 計算，強度越高，效果越強。

    Each weapon randomly has one element. Values marked with * are calculated at 100% Elemental Potency, and the higher the potency, the stronger the effect.

    基礎數值：元素效果機率 15%、元素效果強度 50%。使用信仰遠程技能「箭矢附魔」時，暴擊率會全部轉成元素機率，暴擊傷害則轉成元素強度。

    Base values: Elemental Chance 15%, Elemental Potency 50%. When you use the Faith ranged skill Arrow Infusion, all Crit Rate is converted into Elemental Chance, and Crit Damage is converted into Elemental Potency.

    幽靈 GhostGhost

    對目標附加「Haunted」，2.5 秒內造成額外 120%* 傷害，同時把附近的怪拉過來。同時最多 2 層，再次觸發會擴散到另一隻怪。對一般怪可以無限疊加，對 Boss 每 6 秒重置；怪死掉時，一半的每跳傷害會轉移出去。

    Applies “Haunted” to the target, dealing an extra 120%* damage over 2.5 seconds. It also pulls nearby monsters toward it. It can stack up to 2 times, and triggering it again spreads it to another monster. Against regular monsters it stacks without limit, and against bosses it resets every 6 seconds. When a monster dies, half of its damage per tick is passed on to other targets.

    金屬 MetalMetal

    敵人每層防禦 −3，最多 5 層（−15）。你自己每層減傷 3%，最多 10* 層（30%*），減傷總上限是 80%。

    Enemies get −3 Defense per stack, up to 5 stacks (−15). You reduce damage taken by 3% per stack, up to 10* stacks (30%*), and the total damage reduction cap is 80%.

    火 FireFire

    主目標受到額外 50%* 傷害，附近最多 2 隻各受到 25%*，主目標還會再燃燒 3 秒，共 36%*。

    The main target takes an extra 50%* damage. Up to 2 nearby monsters each take 25%*, and the main target also burns for 3 more seconds, for a total of 36%*.

    雷電 LightningLightning

    在附近最多 4 隻敵人之間彈跳，第一彈 65%*，之後每彈減少 40%（例如 500 → 325 → 130 → 52）。主目標本身不會吃到額外傷害。

    It bounces between up to 4 nearby enemies. The first bounce deals 65%*, and each bounce after that is reduced by 40% (for example, 500 → 325 → 130 → 52). The main target itself does not take the extra damage.

    普通 NormalNormal

    對目標造成額外 80%* 傷害，沒有其他附加效果。

    Deals an extra 80%* damage to the target, with no other effects.

    冰 IceIce

    對目標附加「Frozen」6* 秒並緩速，每層 10%，最多 3 層（30%）。處於冰凍狀態的怪被擊殺時，附近最多 2 隻會吃到 50% 的累積傷害；擴散出去的冰凍傷害會降為四分之一。

    Applies “Frozen” to the target for 6* seconds and slows it by 10% per stack, up to 3 stacks (30%). When a frozen monster is killed, up to 2 nearby monsters take 50% of the accumulated damage. Frozen damage that spreads out is reduced to one quarter.

    水 WaterWater

    稍微緩速目標，並立刻回復你的 HP 和 MP，範圍內最多 2 名隊友也會一起回復。多段攻擊的技能共用一次回復。

    Slightly slows the target and immediately restores your HP and MP. Up to 2 party members in range are restored as well. Skills that hit multiple times share one restore.

    染色與外觀Dyes & Looks

    一般染料Standard Dyes

    • 只能用在標示「可染色」的裝備上，時裝（披風、光環、武器外觀）也包含在內
    • 裝備有幾個顏色欄位，就能各染一種顏色
    • 商城 150 寶石的是自選色，可以直接輸入色碼
    • 鍊金 Lv6 可以自己做，配方由怪物掉落，不同的怪掉不同的顏色
    • 寵物可以用稜彩染料（Prismatic Dye）染色
    • They can only be used on gear marked “Dyeable”, and this includes cosmetics (capes, auras, and weapon skins).
    • Gear with several color slots lets you dye each slot a different color.
    • The 150-gem dye from the Cash Shop is a custom color, and you can enter a color code directly.
    • You can make it yourself with Alchemy Lv6. The recipes drop from monsters, and different monsters drop different colors.
    • Pets can be dyed with Prismatic Dye.

    漸層券（商城）Gradient Tickets (Cash Shop)

    動態漸層Animated Gradient

    • 買了就永久學會，不會用完
    • 只能用在頭髮、眼睛、臉部、嘴巴、身體，裝備不行
    • 要先擁有那個部位的顏色，才能調和
    • 不能跟染料搭配使用，也不能交易
    • Once you buy it, you learn it permanently, and it is not used up.
    • They can only be used on hair, eyes, face, mouth, and body, not on gear.
    • You need to already have a color on that body part before you can blend.
    • They cannot be used together with dyes, and they cannot be traded.
